Instructions

01Choose a wardrobe and outfit

Choose an outfit you like in the wardrobe. Click a wardrobe name to switch collections, or click an outfit cover to play its video.

Switching wardrobes only browses the cards; the current outfit and dialogue continue. Only clicking an outfit cover changes the outfit. “Return to playback” finds the currently playing outfit. Each wardrobe remembers its own collection playback mode.

Next to the wardrobe title, 「Ascending／Descending」 changes the order, remembered separately for each wardrobe. Unavailable outfits always remain at the end and card numbers do not change. Sequential playback follows the chosen order starting with the next outfit; an outfit change already underway finishes first.

02Change playback mode

Click the playback-mode button at the bottom (it shows 「Still mode」) by default) to choose a mode:

Still mode
The video stops at the set time and holds the frame.
Animated companion
Plays the entrance once, then continuously loops an idle animation. Outfits marked as supporting Animated Companion mode can use it; clicking their covers enables it automatically, or you can choose another mode manually.
Loop this outfit
Repeatedly plays the full video for the current outfit.
Play this collection
Loops available outfits in the current wardrobe.
Collection favorites
Loops favorited outfits in the current wardrobe.
Play all collections
Loops all available outfits across every wardrobe.
All favorites
Loops favorited outfits across every wardrobe.

Play All Collections and All Favorites are remembered separately and do not change each wardrobe’s existing collection mode. “This collection” in the menu means the currently playing wardrobe.

At the top of the menu, choose 「In order／Random」 for Play This Collection, Collection Favorites, Play All Collections, and All Favorites. Sequential playback is the default. Random mode shuffles each round without repeats within a round and avoids the same outfit at the boundary between rounds (if there is only one outfit, it continues playing). Your choice is remembered and the current video is preserved when switching; if an outfit change is in progress, the choice takes effect after it finishes.

On first launch and after restoring defaults, the classic layout opens with a still frame, not immersive mode. Click the circular replay button to replay the entrance; while playing, the same button pauses or resumes.

03Favorites and random outfits

Click the heart beside an outfit to favorite it; click again to remove it. After favoriting, choose 「Collection favorites」 to play favorites in this collection, or choose 「All favorites」 to play favorites across wardrobes.

点击「Leave it to your heart」 randomly selects an outfit. Choose 「All in this collection」 to select from all available outfits in the current wardrobe; choose 「Favorites in this collection」 to select only from favorited outfits in this collection.

Favorites are added to the relevant playback queue immediately; if there is only one, it repeats. Removing the last favorite returns playback to Still mode. Favorite modes are unavailable when there are no favorites. During All Favorites, manually selecting a non-favorited standard outfit exits the carousel and switches to Still mode; selecting an Animated Companion outfit enables Animated Companion mode.

How do I adjust playback if it stutters?

Quality options reflect the levels actually provided by the work. The base video is labeled with its actual resolution; if no other levels are available, only the base video is shown. Changing quality preserves playback progress and pause state.

Customize the still frame

Click 「Choose a still frame」 at the bottom, click the timeline to position the frame, and use 「− / ＋」 to fine-tune frame by frame. Then click 「Keep this moment」 to save. You can also drag the timeline in a browser.

After saving, playback switches to Still mode，并记住当前穿搭的Still-frame time .点击「Restore recommendation」 and save again to restore the recommended still frame.

Hide and show the interface

Enable 「自动收起界面」 and the interface hides after 15 seconds of inactivity; move the mouse to show it again.

点击「Immerse」 manually hides the interface. Click 「Open wardrobe」 in the lower-right corner to show it again. Manual immersive mode and exiting it are remembered; pausing and resuming does not change your choice.

In Wallpaper Engine wallpaper settings, 「启动时界面」 can be set to 「记住上次状态」 or 「默认Immerse」或「默认显示」. The default is to remember the previous state. If a default state is specified, each reload opens in that state, but you can still show or hide the interface freely during the current session.

When does Animated Companion dialogue change?

Outfits with dedicated dialogue say one line during entrance and another once settled; afterward, the dialogue changes about every {{dialogueInterval}} seconds. Dialogue continues cycling silently while the interface is hidden; when it reappears, you see the current line, without replaying missed lines. Outfits without dedicated dialogue remain quietly companionable.

Ongoing dialogue pauses when you enter immersive mode manually, pause playback, open settings, or edit a still frame; timing restarts when you resume. Responses to actions such as favoriting or changing outfits take priority.

Sound and display settings

After first use or restoring defaults, sound is enabled by default at volume 35. Click the speaker at the bottom, or toggle 「Play sound」 in settings and adjust the volume; your choice is remembered. Video sound does not play while a still frame is held or playback is paused.

The settings button is in the upper-right corner of the classic layout and at the bottom of the compact layout. You can switch between classic and compact layouts under 「Interface layout」 and adjust Text size or Video quality and Image brightness . Under 「Personalization」 at the bottom of settings, adjust 界面位置 and Aspect ratio and toggle 「Decorative light strip」 .「Fit entire image」 preserves the full image; 「Fill screen」 may crop some edges.

What if desktop icons cover the interface?

In the classic layout, expand 「Personalization」 in settings to show the left alignment guide and playback-control outline and adjust their positions. Use 「Move the left-side interface horizontally」 to move the entire left column; use 「Move playback controls vertically」 to move the middle group from the Play button to “Choose a still frame.” Preview with the arrows or slider, then click 「Done」 to save; Cancel restores the original position. The guides are hidden when Personalization is collapsed or settings are closed. The compact layout stays fixed in the lower-right corner.

To restore the default position, click 「Restore defaults」 and then click 「Done」 .

Timed outfit changes in settings, choose 「Change outfits occasionally」中 directly, or choose 15 seconds / 5 minutes / 15 minutes，选择「Off」 to stop automatic outfit changes. You can also choose 「Custom」 , enter an integer, select seconds or minutes, and click 「Apply」 to apply it; the button turns gray and reads “Applied.” Apply again after changing the value or unit. The minimum is 1 second and the maximum is 60 minutes. The active interval appears to the right of the heading; unapplied input does not change it. Supported modes are Still mode and Animated companion . It pauses during single-outfit looping and carousels. In Still mode, timing starts when the video holds on a frame; entrance and exit animation time is excluded. For standard outfits with an exit animation, the exit plays from the current still before the next outfit appears; manual selections still respond immediately. If already holding a frame, changing the interval restarts the timer when applied. Automatic changes use the current random-selection range and do not reveal the interface.

Timed changes in Animated Companion mode selects only outfits in this collection that support Animated Companion. With “Favorites in this collection,” only favorited animated outfits are selected. Companion time starts after the entrance; when time is up, the current cycle finishes before changing outfits, so the change may occur a little later than scheduled. The new outfit plays its entrance before resuming Animated Companion. Timing pauses during playback pause, host pause, or while using settings, still-frame editing, or the playback menu; missed changes are not made after resuming. Outfit changes can happen silently while the interface is hidden or immersive. Changes pause if the selected range contains no animated outfits, and the outfit remains unchanged if it is the only one in the range.

Restore initial stateclears favorites and custom still frames and restores this work’s default settings.
